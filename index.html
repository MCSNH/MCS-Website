Getting Started

Two ways to find your voice this season

New singers

Auditions are held individually with Music Director Dan Perkins — a brief, friendly conversation about your voice, plus a short excerpt from this semester's music.

Returning singers

Returning members audition September 7–8, 2026, ahead of the season's first rehearsal — everyone sings a short excerpt from this semester's music.

Not sure yet?

Come to Open Sing on September 14 — no audition, no commitment, just an evening of singing together.
