Join Manchester Choral Society

Whether you're new to MCS or coming back for another season, here's everything you need to get started.

Key Dates

This season's timeline

Sep 7–8

Screening — returning singers

Returning members complete a brief vocal screening to confirm or adjust their voice part ahead of the first rehearsal — everyone sings a short excerpt from this semester's music. See screening details →

Sep 14

Open Sing

Monday, 7–8:30pm at Grace Episcopal Church, 106 Lowell Street, Manchester, NH. A no-commitment first sing-through of our December concert music — Poulenc's Gloria and Saint-Saëns' Christmas Oratorio. No audition required.

Ongoing

New singer auditions

New singers audition individually with Music Director Dan Perkins — a friendly conversation about your voice, plus a short excerpt from this semester's music. See what to expect →

Schedule your audition or screening

Once you've submitted the registration form, the next step is scheduling your vocal audition (new singers) or screening (returning singers) — a short, friendly conversation about your voice, not a high-pressure tryout.
