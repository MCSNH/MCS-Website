What to Wear

Concert Dress

Modest and elegant, all black attire:

  • Dress slacks, dress pants — no jeans
  • Skirts and dresses (long, but floor-length not required)
  • Blouses, long-sleeved button-up dress shirts
  • Tops should have sleeves or be worn with a jacket/sweater (no off-the-shoulder, no bare arms, no cleavage, no bare belly)
  • Black stockings, tights, hose, or socks (no white socks)
  • Black shoes — no open-toed shoes. Comfortable shoes encouraged, as long as they're in good condition
  • Tasteful jewelry and bling encouraged
