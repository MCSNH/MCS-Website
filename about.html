Our story

Sixty-five years of community, music, and voices raised together in Manchester, New Hampshire.

Since 1961

A choir built by its community

Manchester Choral Society was founded in 1961 and has brought singers together in Manchester, New Hampshire, for 65 seasons. What started as a small community chorus has grown into a lasting musical home for singers of every background — built, season after season, by the members who show up to rehearsal.
